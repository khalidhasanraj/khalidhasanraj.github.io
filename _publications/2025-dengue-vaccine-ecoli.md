This work designed a pan-serotype, multi-epitope vaccine candidate against dengue virus using immunoinformatics approaches, then cloned the construct into an expression vector, expressed it recombinantly in *E. coli*, and purified the protein using chromatographic techniques. Expression and purity were confirmed by SDS-PAGE and Western blotting.
