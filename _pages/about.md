I am a Lecturer in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Dhaka, where I joined the faculty in May 2026. My research centers on vaccine development and translational medicine.

Research Interests
======
My work integrates computational approaches with wet-lab techniques including recombinant protein expression, purification, and immunological validation, to develop cost-effective, pan-serotype vaccine candidates against viruses such as dengue and monkeypox. I am also engaged in antiviral drug discovery, including the identification of natural compound inhibitors against emerging viruses such as Nipah virus, and in multi-omics bioinformatics projects developing risk-prediction models for stomach and bladder cancer, alongside genetic association studies of non-communicable diseases such as breast and lung cancer in the Bangladeshi population.

Background
======
I completed my B.S. (2023) and M.S. (2025) in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Dhaka, ranking first in my class in both degrees. My master's thesis, supervised by Professor A.H.M. Nurun Nabi, focused on harnessing *E. coli* for the cost-effective production and purification of a novel pan-serotype multi-epitope vaccine against dengue virus.

Prior to joining the faculty, I worked as a Research Fellow and Senior Research Assistant at the International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh (icddr,b), where I contributed to mammalian cell and virus culture, immunological and virological assays, biosimilar potency testing, and antiviral drug discovery in support of clinical vaccine trials.

Teaching
======
As a Lecturer, I teach courses in Biochemistry, Bioinformatics, Molecular Biology, and Nutrition, and am involved in question preparation, exam evaluation, and mentoring undergraduate students in laboratory-based and computational research projects.
