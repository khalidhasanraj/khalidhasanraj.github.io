Research Interests
======
My work integrates computational approaches with wet-lab techniques to solve health problems related to communicable and non-communicable diseases.

A few areas I'm focused on:

* **Vaccine design** : Multi-epitope, pan-serotype candidates against viruses such as dengue and monkeypox
* **Antiviral drug discovery** : Identifying natural compound inhibitors against emerging viruses such as Nipah virus
* **Cancer genetics** : Multi-omics bioinformatics and risk-prediction models for stomach and bladder cancer
* **Disease genetics** : Genetic association studies of non-communicable diseases, including breast and lung cancer, in the Bangladeshi population

Background
======
I completed my B.S. (2023) and M.S. (2025) in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Dhaka, ranking first in my class in both degrees. My master's thesis, supervised by Professor A.H.M. Nurun Nabi, focused on harnessing *E. coli* for the cost-effective production and purification of a novel pan-serotype multi-epitope vaccine against dengue virus.

Prior to joining the faculty, I worked as a Research Fellow and Senior Research Assistant at the International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh (icddr,b), where I contributed to mammalian cell and virus culture, immunological and virological assays, biosimilar potency testing, and antiviral drug discovery in support of clinical vaccine trials.
